# EV Maintenance Schedule Basics

Electric vehicles generally require less routine maintenance than gasoline
vehicles, since they have no engine oil, spark plugs, timing belt, or exhaust
system to service. That said, EVs still have several components that need
periodic attention.

Common EV maintenance items include:

- **Tire rotation and wear checks**, typically every 7,500-10,000 miles. EVs
  are often heavier than comparable gas vehicles due to battery weight, and
  instant torque can accelerate tire wear, so tires may need replacement more
  frequently than on a similar gas vehicle.
- **Brake fluid checks**, generally on a similar interval to gas vehicles.
  Brake pads themselves often last significantly longer on EVs, however,
  because regenerative braking handles much of the deceleration and reduces
  wear on the physical brake pads.
- **Cabin air filter replacement**, typically every 12,000-15,000 miles or
  annually, similar to gas vehicles.
- **Coolant system checks** for the battery thermal management system, on an
  interval that varies significantly by manufacturer — some recommend checks
  every few years, others tie it to specific mileage intervals.

Because EV maintenance schedules and intervals vary meaningfully by
manufacturer, owners should follow the specific schedule in their vehicle's
owner's manual rather than relying on general guidance like this.
