# Home Charging Setup

Most EV owners do the majority of their charging at home overnight, using
either the Level 1 cable included with the vehicle or a dedicated Level 2 home
charging unit.

**Level 1 (no installation required):** Plug the included cable into any
standard 120V outlet. No electrician or installation needed, but charging speed
is limited to roughly 3-5 miles of range per hour, which may not fully replenish
a long commute overnight.

**Level 2 (recommended for most owners):** Requires installing a 240V circuit,
typically similar to what's used for an electric dryer or oven, and either a
dedicated wall-mounted charging unit or a NEMA 14-50 outlet paired with the
vehicle's portable charger. Installation generally requires a licensed
electrician and, in many cases, a permit from the local municipality. Costs
for a home Level 2 installation commonly range from $500 to $2,000 depending on
the distance from the electrical panel, whether a panel upgrade is needed, and
local labor rates.

Many utility companies offer off-peak electricity rate plans specifically for
EV charging, which can significantly reduce the cost of overnight charging —
it's worth checking with a local utility provider before committing to a
charging setup.
