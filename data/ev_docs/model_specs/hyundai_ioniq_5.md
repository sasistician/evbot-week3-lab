# Hyundai Ioniq 5

*Specs below are illustrative, based on publicly known figures for the Ioniq 5
rear-wheel-drive Long Range trim, and may not reflect the current model year.
Treat as a reference point, not a live spec sheet.*

The Hyundai Ioniq 5 is a midsize electric crossover built on Hyundai's dedicated
E-GMP EV platform. The rear-wheel-drive Long Range configuration offers an
EPA-estimated range of approximately 303 miles, with a 0-60 mph time of around
7.4 seconds; the dual-motor AWD version trades some range for a 0-60 time
closer to 5 seconds.

The Ioniq 5 seats five and features a flat floor (a benefit of the dedicated EV
platform) that opens up rear legroom, plus a "universal island" center console
that can slide fore and aft. Cargo space is approximately 27.2 cubic feet
behind the rear seats.

Starting price is approximately $42,000-$47,000 before incentives. The Ioniq 5
supports 800V DC fast charging, enabling a 10%-80% charge in around 18 minutes
under ideal conditions — notably fast for its class.
