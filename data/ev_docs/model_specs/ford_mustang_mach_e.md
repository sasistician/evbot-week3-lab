# Ford Mustang Mach-E

*Specs below are illustrative, based on publicly known figures for the Mach-E
Premium extended-range trim, and may not reflect the current model year. Treat
as a reference point, not a live spec sheet.*

The Ford Mustang Mach-E is a five-passenger electric SUV that borrows styling
cues from Ford's Mustang sports car line. The extended-range rear-wheel-drive
configuration offers an EPA-estimated range of approximately 250-260 miles, with
a 0-60 mph time of around 5.8 seconds. Battery capacity is approximately 88 kWh
usable.

Cargo space totals roughly 29 cubic feet behind the rear seats, plus a small
front trunk for charging cables and accessories. The interior features a
large portrait-oriented touchscreen for infotainment and climate controls.

Starting price is approximately $40,000-$48,000 before incentives, depending on
trim and battery pack. The Mach-E supports DC fast charging at Electrify
America and other CCS-compatible stations, with typical fast-charge sessions
adding around 61 miles of range in 10 minutes under ideal conditions.
