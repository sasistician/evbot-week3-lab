# Tesla Model 3

*Specs below are illustrative, based on publicly known figures for the Model 3
Long Range trim, and may not reflect the current model year. Treat as a
representative reference point, not a live spec sheet.*

The Tesla Model 3 is a compact all-electric sedan. The Long Range rear-wheel-drive
configuration offers an EPA-estimated range of approximately 272 miles on a full
charge, with a 0-60 mph time of around 5.8 seconds. Battery capacity is
approximately 75 kWh usable.

Seating is for five, with a minimalist interior centered on a single central
touchscreen for most vehicle controls. Cargo capacity includes both a rear trunk
and a front trunk ("frunk"), totaling around 23 cubic feet combined.

Starting price for the Long Range trim is approximately $40,000-$45,000 before
incentives, varying by year and options package. The Model 3 supports DC fast
charging at Tesla Supercharger stations, with typical charging sessions adding
significant range in under 30 minutes.
