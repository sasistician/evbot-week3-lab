# Chevrolet Bolt EUV

*Specs below are illustrative, based on publicly known figures for the Bolt EUV,
and may not reflect the current model year or production status. Treat as a
reference point, not a live spec sheet.*

The Chevrolet Bolt EUV is a compact all-electric crossover positioned as one of
the more affordable EVs on the market. It offers an EPA-estimated range of
approximately 247-259 miles on a full charge, with a 0-60 mph time of around
7 seconds — modest compared to sportier EVs, reflecting its focus on
affordability over performance.

The Bolt EUV seats five and offers roughly 16.3 cubic feet of cargo space
behind the rear seats, expandable to around 56.9 cubic feet with seats folded.
It includes GM's Super Cruise hands-free driving assist as an available option
on some trims.

Starting price is approximately $27,000-$30,000 before incentives, making it one
of the lower-cost entry points into EV ownership. The Bolt EUV supports DC fast
charging, with a charging session typically adding around 95 miles of range in
30 minutes under ideal conditions.
