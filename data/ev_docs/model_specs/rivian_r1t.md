# Rivian R1T

*Specs below are illustrative, based on publicly known figures for a
representative R1T configuration, and may not reflect the current model year.
Treat as a reference point, not a live spec sheet.*

The Rivian R1T is an all-electric pickup truck aimed at both off-road and daily
driving use. The quad-motor configuration (one motor per wheel) offers an
EPA-estimated range of approximately 270-314 miles depending on battery pack
size, with a 0-60 mph time of roughly 3 seconds thanks to independent motor
control at each wheel.

The R1T seats five and includes a covered bed with a "gear tunnel" storage
compartment running behind the cab — a feature unique to Rivian's truck
platform. Towing capacity is rated up to 11,000 lbs on quad-motor
configurations.

Starting price is approximately $70,000-$90,000 before incentives, depending on
battery pack and motor configuration. The R1T supports DC fast charging at
Rivian Adventure Network stations and other CCS-compatible networks.
